HOW TO PLAY

THE GAME

Every question has many correct answers. The fewer players who give your answer, the more points it earns.

Everyone gets the same 7 questions each day. You have 25 seconds for each and one answer per question.

Wrong answers score zero. The rarity of an answer stays hidden until you lock yours in.

SCORING

Very common (10%+ of players)10
Common (4-10%)25
Uncommon (1.5-4%)50
Rare (0.5-1.5%)100
Extremely rare (under 0.5%)200

Tiers come from how often each answer is given. In this demo the field is simulated.
